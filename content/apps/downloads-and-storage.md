Playing a book is as simple as tapping **Play**. Behind the scenes, ListenUp downloads and saves the file to your device, so the next time you open that book it starts instantly, and plays even with no connection.

> [!NOTE]
> This page describes the **phone apps**. The [web client](/apps/web/) streams instead — a browser cannot hold your library on the device, so there are no downloads to manage there.

## Downloading for offline

Next to each book's Play button is a **download** control that shows the book's download status. Once the book is fully downloaded, it turns into a **delete** button.

If you delete a download and then play the book again, ListenUp takes the hint: it assumes you'd rather not keep that one on your device, so it streams it instead of silently re-downloading. Want it offline again? Just tap the **download** icon.

> [!TIP]
> Heading somewhere without a connection? Download what you want to hear first. Anything already on your device plays fully offline.

## Wi-Fi only

Downloads wait for Wi-Fi by default, and a waiting book says so. To allow downloads on mobile data, turn off **Download on Wi-Fi only** in Settings.

## Managing storage

The **Storage** page shows how much space ListenUp is using and lists every book downloaded to your device. Delete one book from there, or clear them all. A book that's playing can't be deleted until you stop it.

- **Android**: **Settings → Storage → Manage storage**.
- **iOS**: the **Downloads** row on your profile page, or **Settings → Downloads → Manage storage**.

## When you finish a book

A finished book stays downloaded until you delete it. Finishing does reset the "don't download this one" hint from above, so if you start the book again later it downloads as usual.
