## Home

Home is about you. From the top:

{{< webshot src="web-home.png" alt="Home in the web client, with books in progress, the last seven days of listening, and shelves" caption="Home, in the web client" >}}

- **One card that changes with where you are.** Just finished a book? It asks how it was and offers the next one in the series. Nothing in progress? It suggests the next book in a series you're reading. Brand new? It points you at something to start.
- **Everything in progress**: every book you've started and not finished.
- **Your last 7 days**: a chart of your listening, your current and best streak, your top genres, and a link to the leaderboard.
- **My shelves**: your [shelves](/library/shelves/), with a shortcut to make a new one.

## Library

The Library has four tabs: **Books**, **Series**, **Authors** and **Narrators**.

**Sort.** Books sort by title, author, length, year published, recently added, rating, your listeners' rating, or series. Series sort by name, number of books or recently added. People sort by name or number of books.

**Filter.** **Show** narrows Books to **In progress**, **Not started** or **Finished**. It goes back to **All** each time you open the app.

**Jump to a letter.** When the list is sorted alphabetically, an index down the side jumps straight to a letter.

**Act on a book without opening it.** Long-press a cover (right-click on the web) for Play or Resume, **Add to shelf**, **Share** and **Select**. Admins also get **Add to collection** and **Edit details**.

**Select several.** Choose **Select**, tap the books you want, then add them all to a shelf at once. **Select all** takes the whole list.

## Search

Search looks across **books**, **people**, **series** and **tags**, and you can narrow the results to any one of them. It starts after three characters.

Search runs on your device, against the library the app already holds. That makes it instant, and it means it works exactly the same with no connection.

On the web, press `/` (or `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) to search from anywhere.
