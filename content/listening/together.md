If you're the only person on your server, all of this stays out of the way. It gets interesting when you invite someone.

## Discover

Discover is about everyone else. From the top:

- **Listening now**: who has a book playing this minute, followed by what others were most recently listening to over the last two weeks.
- **Something new**: ten books you haven't started, picked so that a series starts at its first book. **Shuffle** deals another ten.
- **Leaderboard** and **Activity** (below).
- **Shelves by others**: public shelves, grouped by who made them.
- **Recently added**: the ten newest books on the server.

## Activity

The feed records the moments worth knowing about: someone **started** or **finished** a book, **listened** to part of one, reached a **listening streak** or a total-hours milestone, **created a shelf**, or **joined the server**. **See all activity** opens the last hundred entries.

Two rules keep it honest:

- **A listen has to be real.** Opening a book for a few seconds doesn't announce anything. It takes at least a minute in one sitting.
- **You only see what you can see.** Activity about a book you don't have access to never reaches you. See [Collections](/admin/collections/).

## Leaderboard

Rank everyone by **Time**, **Books** or **Streak**, over a **Week**, **Month**, **Year** or **All time**. The streak board ranks each person's longest streak, not their current one.

## On a book's page

- **Who's reading it.** The top of the page says who is listening now and who has finished.
- **Where they are.** A chapter shows the avatars of anyone currently on it, lit while they're actually listening.
- **Readers.** Everyone who has started the book, with how far along they are or when they finished, and their rating if they left one.

## Ratings

Rate any book out of five: tap a star, or drag for half stars. You can add a short note of up to 280 characters.

A book's page shows three things, yours first:

- **Your rating.**
- **ListenUp score**: one number combined from outside sources. Depending on the book that's Audible, Goodreads and Hardcover.
- **Your listeners**: how the people on your server rated it.

## Profiles

Tap anyone's name to see their profile: how much they've listened, how many books they've finished, their current and best streak, the last books they listened to, and their public shelves.

## What others can see of you {#privacy}

Worth knowing before you invite people: listening on a shared server is visible to its members.

- **Visible to everyone on the server:** what you're listening to and how far you are, your finishes, streaks and totals, your ratings and notes, and your public shelves.
- **Yours to hide:** any shelf, by making it private.
- **Hidden by access:** a book someone can't access is invisible to them everywhere, including your activity about it.

There's no setting to hide your own activity or leave the leaderboard. If a book should stay between you and the server's admins, an admin can put it in a [private collection](/admin/collections/).
